# UI-011 · SHEAR

Limb becomes an exploded airframe. Three mineral blades in **Saturated**, five in **Insane**, cut through the annular workspace instrument. Faceted bay housings dock against a fixed datum; the workspace number, command input and service controls remain readable and immediate.

Motion has a cause: startup assembles the structure once; workspace changes articulate its blades and index arm; focus changes briefly strain the leading edge; opening, replacing and closing a bay docks or releases its plane. Everything settles. No loops, visual timers, shaders, blur, or extra service demand. Reduced motion removes travel; off and low-effects stop animation. Hidden hosts settle their moving parts.

**Biggest weakness found:** the rear blades crossed the window register. I narrowed and moved the sculpture, restoring a clear text column without flattening its depth.

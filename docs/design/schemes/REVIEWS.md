# UI-015 research and independent visual review

**Accepted after one revision.** The final compact picker opens with the active Glacial specimen fully visible, including its name, inverse selection sample, description and all six bands. The two actionable findings below are corrected. This record covers research, palette propagation and picker presentation; the task's complete test and scenario results are recorded separately in the README.

The owner's “serpanitum” is confidently identified as [Serpantinum](https://serpantinum.dev/), a Wayland shell. Its [actual ThemeTab source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilyamiro/serpantinum/master/src/quickshell/guide/theme/ThemeTab.qml) matches the request precisely: compact three-column tiles show each scheme's own background and text, its name and six colour dots. Stronger text and an outline identify selection. A click persists the palette and applies it live, including app-template generation. The useful principle is immediate comparison of real surface and accent relationships. AEROLITH carries that principle into specimens with selection samples, ordered bands and separate cursor corners.

Other primary references informed the review: [KDE Colors](https://docs.kde.org/stable_kf6/en/plasma-workspace/kcontrol/colors/index.html) documents previews and distinct selection/focus roles; [VS Code themes](https://code.visualstudio.com/docs/configure/themes) supports keyboard preview and confirmation; [kitty themes](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/kittens/themes/) combines live previews with search and recently used choices. The instrument register is AEROLITH's design interpretation, rather than a reproduction of these interfaces.

A smaller width concern was corrected during construction: two columns originally began at 430 design units, leaving too little name space around the index and active-state label. The threshold is now 600. The reviewed compact and native frames preserve readable scheme names.

The twelve names fit the mineral, optical and scientific language of AEROLITH. The catalogue offers useful green, petrol, indigo, copper, rose, violet, neutral, olive, gold, pink, ceramic and ice treatments. Cinder/Vellum and Iris/Umbra remain distinguishable through their planes and interaction accents. Independent calculation checked 216 text/status pairs across the three opaque planes: all pass 4.5:1, with a minimum of **5.35:1** for Helios critical text against `surface2`. The additional 36 PIYTI-link pairs reach at least **6.86:1**, for Lichen against `surface2`. The task's expanded contrast test also covers composited planes, inverse selection and focus.

Stable status colours preserve their meaning across the catalogue. Gold interaction in Helios, rose interaction in Ferrite and green interaction in Lichen are close to corresponding status hues, so the explicit band legend, ACTIVE word, selected rim and cursor corners matter. The reviewed picker supplies these independent cues. [The native intermediate frame](picker/review-cursor-limb-active-glacial.png) demonstrates separate states: despite that development filename, its actual content is **cursor Limb, active Glacial**. Compact Home/End, application and register frames also show usable navigation and readable samples.

The propagation audit found palette-change repaint hooks in InstrumentField, ShearField, Bezel, ChordSlot, InstrumentTabs, CircularField, Deck elevation and LockView. OSD and Session bind Theme roles directly. PIYTI's formerly fixed cyan edge now resolves the chosen specimen's link; THEARS uses its signal role. The allowed shell visuals contain no remaining fixed opaque palette colours. Custom palette overrides remain active and are explicitly disclosed in the picker.

The picker adds no timer, Canvas, shader, layer or ambient job. Its displayed strings use plain text, its actions use the existing validated Settings mutation path, and its cursor and applied scheme remain independent. No source or visual blocker remains within this review's scope. Native compositor/GPU behaviour and external application activation require the separate systems work described in SYSTEMS-REQUESTS.md.

Final evidence spot review inspected all twelve 1080p grounds, representative frames for every bay and notices, and eight native 4K frames spanning both directions and intensities. No new visual, contrast or coverage blocker was found. The unscaled THEARS/Saturated set intentionally has smaller effective type than the scale-2 sets. No rendering process was started during the CPU measurements.
